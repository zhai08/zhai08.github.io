About

A scientist in training, moving between benchwork and computation.

My work has grown from synthetic biology into chemical biology, bacteriophage-host defense, cell polarity, and reproducible data analysis. I am drawn to questions that need both experimental care and quantitative imagination: how fusion oncoproteins can be targeted, how cellular inhibitors can be profiled, how phages negotiate bacterial immunity, and how cellular architecture holds itself together. I first came to life science through a long-horizon fascination with longevity: whether aging and life-limiting disease could become measurable, targetable, and eventually treatable through rigorous biology, chemical biology, and drug discovery.
